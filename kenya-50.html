How to play Kenya @ 50

  1. The host creates a game and teams. Everyone else joins from their phone with the room code or QR code and a nickname, then picks a team and marker.
  2. Teams take turns. On your turn, roll the die and move that many tiles clockwise from START.
  3. Land on a black tile and you play a red card. Land on a red tile and you play a black card.
  4. The describer (players rotate within the team) is the only one who sees the 11 words. They have 50 seconds to describe as many as they can without saying them. They may swap the card up to 2 times.
  5. When time is up the card is revealed to everyone. The describer marks the guessed words and every other player must approve the count.
  6. Each word is worth 10 points. First team to 500 points wins.
  7. Judiciary tiles draw a judiciary card, which can cost or win points, or give a saved get-out-of-jail-free card.
  8. Special tiles only apply their effect: Mara (back 3), Rhino (forward 4), Mombasa (skip a turn), SGR (+10), Give 10 / Steal 10 from any team. Every pass of START pays 50 points.
