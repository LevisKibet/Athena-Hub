How to play Scrabble

  1. The host creates a game and shares the room code or QR code. 2 to 4 players join with a nickname.
  2. Everyone gets 7 tiles that only they can see. The first word must cover the centre star.
  3. On your turn, tap a tile in your rack, then tap a square. Tap a placed tile to take it back. A blank tile asks which letter it stands for.
  4. Tiles go in one row or column and every word must connect to the tiles already on the board. The score preview shows what you will get before you play.
  5. Premium squares only count on the turn a tile is placed: double and triple letter, double and triple word. Using all 7 tiles in one turn is a 50 point bingo.
  6. Words are not checked when you play. The next player can challenge before their move. A word that is not in the dictionary is taken back and its points are removed. If every word is valid, the challenger loses a turn.
  7. You can exchange tiles while at least 7 remain in the bag, or pass. Six turns in a row with no score end the game.
  8. The game ends when the bag is empty and a player uses their last tile. Leftover tile values are subtracted from each other player and added to the player who went out.
